Support

Having a problem or an idea? Write to {{ site.eposta }}. Please include your device model (e.g. iPhone 16, Samsung Galaxy A54) and what you were doing when the problem happened; a screenshot helps a lot.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play?
Swipe left / right: change lanes and turn at corners. Swipe up: jump. Swipe down: slide. Collect coins and dodge obstacles and the animals chasing you.

Where is my progress saved?
Only on your device. If you uninstall the game or move to a new phone, coins, gems, costumes and records are not transferred.

My purchase is missing.
Reopen the game while online. For one-time packs (Starter Pack, Ad-free Rewards) tap Gems > Previous purchases > RESTORE (with the same Apple / Google account). Gem packs are consumable and are not restored on reinstall.

I want a refund.
Payments are processed by Apple and Google; refunds are requested through them: Apple – Report a Problem · Google Play refunds.

How do I change personalised ad consent?
In Settings > Privacy (13 and older). Ads are never personalised for players under 13.

Are ads mandatory?
No. You choose when to watch an ad (continue, 2x coins, daily gift). The "Ad-free Rewards" pack grants these rewards without ads.

I want my data deleted.
We hold no data about you; uninstalling the game is enough. For data held by ad partners see section 6 of the Privacy Policy or write to us.
