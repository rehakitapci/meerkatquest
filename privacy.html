Privacy Policy

Developer: {{ site.gelistirici }} · Contact: {{ site.eposta }}

1. In short

Meerkat Quest is a mobile running game. We (the developer) do not collect your name, e-mail, phone number, location or any other personal information; the game has no server of its own and sends no data to us. Your game progress (coins, gems, record, costumes, settings) is stored only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Ads and payments in the game are processed by the companies named below; the data they process is governed by their own policies.

2. Age screen

On first launch the game asks for your year of birth. It is stored only on your device, is never sent anywhere, and is used only to choose suitable ad and purchase settings. Players under 13, or of unknown age, get child-appropriate settings.

3. Ads

The game only shows optional rewarded ads that the player chooses to watch (e.g. "watch an ad to continue"). There are no forced interstitial ads. Ads are served through Unity's LevelPlay mediation and the ad networks connected to it (e.g. Unity Ads, ironSource Ads).

To show, measure and analyse ads and to prevent fraud, these companies may process: device and advertising identifiers, approximate location derived from your IP address, interactions with ads (e.g. views, clicks) and diagnostic / performance information (e.g. device model, operating system). This data is encrypted in transit.

  • Players under 13 (or of unknown age): ads are not personalised; on Android the advertising ID is not sent; only ad networks suitable for Google Play's Families program are used; ad content is limited to child-appropriate content and the player is flagged as a child to the ad networks (COPPA).
  • Players 13 and older: personalised ads are shown only with your explicit consent in the game. You can withdraw it at any time in Settings > Privacy. You can also turn off ad personalisation in your device settings.
  • iPhone / iPad: the game does not request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission, so your Apple advertising identifier (IDFA) is not shared with ad partners.

Ad partner policy: Unity – Game Player and App User Privacy Policy (Unity Ads and ironSource / LevelPlay).

4. In-app purchases

Gem packs and one-time packs are sold for real money through Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iPhone). We never see your payment details; payments are processed by Google or Apple (Google Privacy Policy, Apple Privacy Policy). Players under 13 see a parental gate before a purchase. Parents can also require approval for purchases with Google Play family settings or Apple's Ask to Buy.

5. Children

The game is suitable for all ages, including children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ad settings for players under 13 are described above; links that leave the game (such as this page) open only after a parental gate. If you have a question about your child, write to {{ site.eposta }}.

6. Your rights and data deletion

  • Your game progress exists only on your device: uninstalling the game deletes all of it.
  • For data processed by ad partners, use the privacy icon in the ad, your device's ad settings, or the options in Unity's policy above.
  • For any question or request about your personal data (including your rights under GDPR and Turkey's KVKK), write to {{ site.eposta }}; we reply within 30 days.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page and the "Last updated" date changes.
